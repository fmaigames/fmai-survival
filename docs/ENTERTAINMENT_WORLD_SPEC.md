# FMAI Survival — Entertainment World Specification

## Social & Recreation

The world includes non-combat social spaces so survival is not the only activity.

### Town Festival
- Festival/carnival grounds
- Food stalls and vendors
- Mini-games and prize booths
- Decorative lights and seasonal props
- Limited-time missions and rewards

### Party System
- Player parties/groups
- Party activities and missions
- Social gathering areas
- Private party/chat spaces

### Enjoyment Park
- Ferris wheel and carousel-style attractions
- Arcade/mini-games
- Picnic and food areas
- Photo/social spots
- Decorative event lighting

## Design Rules
- Entertainment content is optional and must not block survival progression.
- Rewards can include XP, coins, cosmetics, decorations, food, collectibles, and event items.
- Seasonal events can rotate without requiring paid upgrades.
- Multiplayer/social features must support moderation, mute, block, and report controls.
- Use original or properly licensed assets; do not copy proprietary assets from other games.

## Future Integration
- Town Hall social hub
- Public/private transport routes
- Global, town, team, and private chat
- Mission/reward system
- Market and automatic supply safety-net system
- Admin moderation and event controls

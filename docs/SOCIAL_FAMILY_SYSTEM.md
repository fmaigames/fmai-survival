# FMAI Survival — Social & Family System

## Goal

Provide a safe, non-sexual social-life system where players can help one another, form families, marry, and raise children as family NPCs.

## Player-to-player help

- Medical assistance and revival.
- Sharing food, medicine, materials, and equipment.
- Cooperative rescue and family missions.
- Safe social interactions with block/report/moderation controls.

## Marriage

Marriage is a social/family progression feature only. There is no sexual content, sexual interaction, or sexual scene.

Players can form a family partnership and unlock:

- Shared home access.
- Shared storage permissions.
- Farm and kitchen cooperation.
- Family missions.
- Family decorations and activities.

## Children

Children are generated/added through a non-sexual family progression mechanic. The game does not depict or simulate conception.

Children are family NPCs with safe gameplay roles such as:

- Home activities.
- Education.
- Gardening and simple chores.
- Family conversations.
- Safe town and festival activities.

Children never participate in combat or sexual content.

## Safety

- No sexual scenes or sexual interactions.
- No sexualized child content.
- Consent and relationship confirmation for social progression.
- Block/report tools for multiplayer safety.
- Moderation hooks for inappropriate behavior.

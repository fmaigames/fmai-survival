# FMAI Survival — Social World Architecture

## Core Hub
The Town Hall is the central social hub connecting players, services, transport, events and communication.

## Social Spaces
- Town Hall lobby and mission board
- Decorated chat room
- Festival/carnival grounds
- Enjoyment park
- Market and shops
- Public transport stations
- Party gathering areas
- Safe social zones

## Party Flow
1. Player creates or joins a party.
2. Party members can meet in the Town Hall or another safe zone.
3. Party missions and event activities can be started together.
4. Rewards are granted through the existing mission reward system.
5. Players can leave the party without losing personal progression.

## Chat Channels
- Global
- Town
- Party/team
- Private
- Admin announcements

Moderation controls: mute, block, report and server-side audit logging.

## Transport Integration
Public transport connects major world regions. Private vehicles provide flexible travel. Transport should reduce travel friction without making exploration mandatory.

## Events
Festival and enjoyment-park events can be temporary or seasonal. Event rewards should be useful but optional and must not block the main survival progression.

## Safety-Net Rules
- If a player does not trade, the economy must still provide a progression path.
- If inventory is full, rewards go to overflow storage/mailbox rather than being silently lost.
- If a required resource is unavailable, missions provide an alternative source or emergency supply.
- Social activities must never be required to complete the core survival story.

## Backend/Admin Integration
The future backend exposes controlled services for player accounts, parties, chat moderation, events, transport schedules, rewards and audit logs. Admin actions require authorization and are logged.

## Asset Rule
All environments, characters, UI and entertainment assets must be original or properly licensed. Do not copy proprietary assets, maps, characters, UI or story content from other games.
